Script

Pipeline

    A preview of the dubbing workspace. Every line stays editable before the final voice is rendered.

    One upload. Five passes. A new voice.

    You pick the language. Ndimi runs the rest, and you can step in to fix a word or a line at any point before it speaks.

    1. Separate

      Voices are lifted off the music and ambient sound, so the soundtrack survives the dub.

    2. Transcribe

      Speech becomes timed text, split by speaker and sentence.

    3. Translate

      Each line is translated and sized to fit the seconds it has on screen.

    4. Re-voice

      A new voice reads each line, matched to the tone and pace of the original speaker.

    5. Sync

      The new speech is laid back over the picture and mixed with the original sound bed.

    Built from Kenyan languages outward.

    Kenya speaks more than forty languages, and most have no place in speech technology. We start where usable speech data exists and add more as communities help us record.

    Dub first. Then speak live. Then teach.

    Each phase runs on the language data the one before it proves out. Every dubbed video is also a recording of the language, kept for the next generation.
